Galactic dynamics
-------------------

Oort and Lindblad's differential rotation, Oort's and Zwicky's missing
mass from velocity dispersions, Rubin and Ford's flat rotation
curves, the Navarro-Frenk-White halo profile, and Chandrasekhar's dynamical
friction -- the rotation-curve and dark-matter machinery in
:mod:`physicskit.astro.galactic_dynamics`.
