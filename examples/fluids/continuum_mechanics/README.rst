Continuum Mechanics
-------------------

Linear continuum mechanics beyond flow. Fourier's heat equation smooths a
temperature field mode by mode. The explicit, Crank-Nicolson, and spectral
solvers show how stability and accuracy trade off. Sound in a pipe or a
room rings at a discrete set of standing-wave frequencies, recovered here
from a single pressure pulse. A pressurized thick-walled cylinder, solved
with plane-strain finite elements, reproduces Lamé's closed-form stresses.
