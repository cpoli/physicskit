Topology
--------

Topological band theory: SSH edge states and the Zak phase, exactly
quantized Chern numbers (TKNN), the Haldane model's zero-net-flux Chern
insulator and its chiral edge states, unpaired Majorana zero modes in the
Kitaev chain, graphene's massless Dirac cone, and the Kane-Mele/BHZ
:math:`\mathbb{Z}_2` topological insulators.
