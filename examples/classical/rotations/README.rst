Rigid-body rotations
=======================

Free and torqued rigid bodies: the intermediate axis theorem
(Dzhanibekov effect) for a torque-free top, rendered both abstractly
(the SO(3) momentum sphere) and literally (a tumbling 3D body); a heavy
symmetric top's precession and nutation under gravity; Euler's disk
settling into a finite-time singularity; and a rattleback's one-way
spin reversal.
