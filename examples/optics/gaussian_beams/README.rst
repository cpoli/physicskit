Gaussian beams
--------------

Complex-beam-parameter (Gaussian) wave optics: Kogelnik and Li's ABCD
transformation of the complex beam parameter through a thin lens,
Siegman's :math:`M^2` beam-quality factor for real, non-diffraction-limited
beams, and the doughnut intensity and helical phase of an Allen et al.
Laguerre-Gaussian orbital-angular-momentum vortex.
