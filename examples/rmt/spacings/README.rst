Spacings and spectral correlations
----------------------------------

Level repulsion and its consequences: the Wigner surmise, Dyson's circular
ensembles and the sine kernel, the Keating-Snaith moments of the CUE
characteristic polynomial, the Poisson (Berry-Tabor) integrable baseline,
the Pandey-Mehta GOE-GUE crossover, Bohigas-Giannoni-Schmit spectral
rigidity and universality, and the Wigner-Dyson many-body level statistics
of Kitaev's SYK model.
