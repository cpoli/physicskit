Coupled lattice chains
=========================

One-dimensional lattices: the exactly-solvable harmonic chain, the
Fermi-Pasta-Ulam-Tsingou beta-lattice and its famous non-ergodic
recurrence, and the discrete sine-Gordon chain's topological solitons.
