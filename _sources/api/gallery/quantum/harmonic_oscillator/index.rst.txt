Harmonic oscillator
--------------------

Planck's quantized oscillators and the blackbody spectrum, and the
quantum harmonic oscillator suite: Fock states, Glauber coherent
states, squeezed states, thermal broadening, an animated Fock-state
superposition, and the Wigner quasi-probability distribution in phase
space.
