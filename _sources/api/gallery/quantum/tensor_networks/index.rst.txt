    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="The Hilbert space of N spins grows as 2^N, but the physically relevant states of 1D chains carry little entanglement and fit in a matrix product state (MPS) of bond dimension \chi. White&#x27;s DMRG (1992) found ground states this way; Vidal&#x27;s time-evolving block decimation (TEBD, 2003-2004) evolves an MPS in time by splitting e^{-iH\delta t} into two-site gates on even and odd bonds and truncating each bond back to \chi with an SVD. In imaginary time the same algorithm finds ground states.">
