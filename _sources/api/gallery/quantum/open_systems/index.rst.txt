Open quantum systems
--------------------

A qubit that leaks energy and phase into its environment: the Lindblad
master equation's :math:`T_1` relaxation and :math:`T_2` dephasing, and
the Monte Carlo wavefunction method that unravels the same dynamics into
individual jumping quantum trajectories.
