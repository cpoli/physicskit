Potentials
----------

Bound states and scattering in 1D potential wells, and 2D quantum boxes:
the matrix Numerov solver applied to asymmetric wells, the gravitational
"quantum bouncer" (also solved with WKB and Kramers's connection phases), the symmetric double well (including an animated,
complex-valued tunneling oscillation), the finite square well (bound
states, resonant transmission, and real-time animated wavepacket
scattering/tunneling), and rectangular/circular/stadium 2D boxes.
