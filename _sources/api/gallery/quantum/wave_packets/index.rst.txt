Wave packets
------------

Non-stationary wave dynamics: free dispersion (with an animated view of
the spreading), twin-slit interference, quantum revivals in an infinite
well, Ehrenfest's theorem in an anharmonic potential, and a phase-colored
animation of double-well tunneling.
