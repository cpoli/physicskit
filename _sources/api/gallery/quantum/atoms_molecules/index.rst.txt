Atoms and molecules
-------------------

Many-electron quantum mechanics by the self-consistent-field method:
Hartree-Fock energies of the hydrogen molecule and the helium atom, from
the minimal STO-3G basis to the Hartree-Fock limit.

Fock and Slater (1930) added the exchange term required by the antisymmetry of the wavefunction. restricted_hartree_fock solves the resulting Roothaan equations FC = SC\varepsilon. This example follows the SCF iterations for helium, converges helium to the Hartree-Fock limit -2.86168 hartree with growing Gaussian bases, and traces the \mathrm H_2 bond, including the well-known failure of restricted Hartree-Fock at dissociation.">
