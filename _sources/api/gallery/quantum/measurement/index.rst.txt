Measurement
-----------

The Born rule made concrete: simulated projective measurement outcomes
converging to the analytic probability density; the position-momentum
uncertainty bound; and two classic measurement/interference demonstrations
propagated genuinely in time -- the double-slit experiment and Stern-Gerlach
beam splitting.
