Electronic transport
--------------------

Drude's classical electron gas (DC and AC conductivity, the Hall effect)
and the Boltzmann equation with Fermi-Dirac statistics that corrected it
(the Wiedemann-Franz law and the thermopower of a degenerate metal).

Sommerfeld (1927-1928) kept the kinetic picture but used Fermi-Dirac statistics. Only electrons within k_BT of the Fermi energy carry current or heat. The Boltzmann equation in the relaxation-time approximation (~physicskit.condensed.transport.boltzmann_transport) then gives, for any band, the Wiedemann-Franz law \kappa/\sigma T = \pi^2/3 (k_B = e = 1) and Mott&#x27;s formula for the Seebeck coefficient. This example checks both on a 2D tight-binding band sampled on a k-grid.">
