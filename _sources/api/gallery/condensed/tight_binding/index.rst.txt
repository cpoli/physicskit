Tight-Binding
-------------

The generic Bloch-Hamiltonian machinery: recovering the exact tight-binding
dispersion from a real-space chain, a Hofstadter-like spectrum from
Peierls-substituted Landau levels, and a two-orbital Slater-Koster LCAO band
structure.
