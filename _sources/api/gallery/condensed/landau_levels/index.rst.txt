Landau Levels
-------------

The continuum quantization of a charged particle in a uniform magnetic
field: equally spaced Landau levels, their macroscopic per-area
degeneracy, and the disorder-broadened density of states whose filling
factor sets the quantum Hall effect's plateau sequence.
