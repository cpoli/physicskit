Quantum spin chains
-------------------

Exact diagonalization of spin-1/2 chains in their symmetry sectors,
checked against the classic exact solutions: Bethe's ansatz for the
Heisenberg antiferromagnet, the free-fermion solution of the
transverse-field Ising chain, and the Calabrese-Cardy logarithmic
entanglement of a critical chain versus the area law of a gapped one.
