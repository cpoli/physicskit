Detector signatures
----------------------

Curved cloud-chamber tracks and branching cascade showers -- the
signatures that identified the positron, the muon, and cosmic-ray
showers, from :mod:`physicskit.particle.collider`.

If those penetrating particles were electrons, bremsstrahlung should have stopped them; if they were protons, they should have ionized far more heavily at their momentum. Anderson and Neddermeyer (1937) concluded they had intermediate mass.
