Curie and Joliot took it to be gamma rays. In 1932 Chadwick showed the numbers didn&#x27;t work for photons. A photon would need about 55 MeV to give a proton that much recoil in a Compton collision, and it would have to be even more energetic to explain the nitrogen recoils he also measured. The same energy could not account for both. A neutral particle with about the proton&#x27;s mass, colliding elastically, accounts for both recoils with a single energy.">
