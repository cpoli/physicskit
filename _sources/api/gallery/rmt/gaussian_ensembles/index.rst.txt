Gaussian ensembles
------------------

Bulk spectra of Hermitian random matrices: Wigner's semicircle law, the
Two-Body Random Ensemble's Gaussian (not semicircular) many-body density of
states, Anderson localization in power-law banded matrices, the
Altland-Zirnbauer Bogoliubov-de Gennes classes' :math:`\pm E` eigenvalue
symmetry, and the Dumitriu-Edelman tridiagonal model for continuum
:math:`\beta`.
