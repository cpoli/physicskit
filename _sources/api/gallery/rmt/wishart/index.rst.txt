Wishart and Jacobi ensembles
----------------------------

Covariance-type ensembles: Wishart's original sample-covariance
construction, the Marchenko-Pastur law, the Wachter law for Jacobi (MANOVA)
ensembles, and Page's conjecture for the average entanglement entropy of a
random quantum state.
