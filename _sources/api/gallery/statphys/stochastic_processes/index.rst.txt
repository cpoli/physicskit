Stochastic processes
--------------------

Brownian motion and the Einstein relation, Langevin dynamics and the
fluctuation-dissipation balance, the Fokker-Planck equation for the
probability density, the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process, and the
Euler-Maruyama and Milstein schemes that integrate Itô stochastic
differential equations.
