Quantum scars
-----------------

The stadium billiard's bouncing-ball scarred eigenstate family, the
scar-enhancement diagnostic, and a Husimi phase-space view of a scarred
state's wall slice; and Heller's wavepacket-recurrence picture of the
spectrum, with Herman-Kluk propagation in a Morse well.

.. raw:: html

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Heller&#x27;s 1984 argument for scars is a statement about wavepackets. Start a Gaussian packet on a periodic orbit of period T. Each time it comes back around, its overlap with its starting state, the autocorrelation C(t)=\langle\psi_0|\psi(t)\rangle, has a recurrence. The Fourier transform of C(t) is the spectrum weighted by the packet, \sum_n|c_n|^2\delta(E-E_n). Short-time recurrences at multiples of T therefore force that weighted spectrum to bunch into bands spaced 2\pi\hbar/T, so some eigenstates must carry extra weight on the orbit: a scar. Herman and Kluk&#x27;s frozen-Gaussian propagator, published the same year, made such packet dynamics computable semiclassically.">
