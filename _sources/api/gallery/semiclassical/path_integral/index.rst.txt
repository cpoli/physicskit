Feynman paths and the classical limit
------------------------------------------

Feynman's sum-over-histories path integral, sampled by Monte Carlo for the
exactly-solvable free particle and harmonic oscillator, showing the
phasor sum concentrate onto the classical (stationary-action) trajectory
as :math:`\hbar\to 0`.
