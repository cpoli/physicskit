WKB and Bohr-Sommerfeld quantization
---------------------------------------

The classical momentum and turning points, the WKB wavefunction, and the
Bohr-Sommerfeld (EBK) quantization rule, checked against the exact
harmonic-oscillator spectrum and eigenstates; Weyl's law counting levels
by phase-space volume; Einstein's torus quantization of the circular
billiard; and the Langer correction that makes radial WKB exact for
hydrogen.
