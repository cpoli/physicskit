Einstein's torus quantization: the circular billiard
=======================================================

Sommerfeld quantized each separated coordinate on its own,
:math:`\oint p_i\,dq_i = n_i h`. Einstein (1917) pointed out that this
depends on the choice of coordinates, and gave the invariant form: an
integrable motion with :math:`f` degrees of freedom winds around an
:math:`f`-dimensional torus in phase space, and the quantization rule is
one condition per independent closed loop :math:`\gamma_i` on that torus,

.. math::

    I_i = \frac{1}{2\pi}\oint_{\gamma_i}\mathbf p\cdot d\mathbf q
    = \hbar\,(n_i + \alpha_i).

The loop integrals do not change when a loop is deformed on the torus, so
the rule needs no special coordinates. Einstein also noted that a chaotic
orbit, which fills a region instead of a torus, admits no such rule. The
offsets :math:`\alpha_i` were added by Keller in 1958.

The circular billiard of radius :math:`R` shows the torus directly.
An orbit with angular momentum :math:`\hbar m` never comes closer to the
centre than the caustic :math:`r_c = m/k`, and fills the annulus
:math:`r_c<r<R`. The two loops on its torus give

.. math::

    I_\theta = \hbar m,\qquad
    I_r = \frac{\hbar}{\pi}\left[\sqrt{k^2R^2-m^2} - m\arccos\frac{m}{kR}\right]
        = \hbar\left(s-\tfrac14\right),

with :math:`\alpha_r = \tfrac14+\tfrac12` from one caustic and one hard
wall. The exact levels are the Bessel zeros :math:`J_m(kR)=0`.

    import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
    import numpy as np
    from scipy.optimize import brentq
    from scipy.special import jn_zeros

    R = 1.0


    def radial_action(x, m):
        """I_r / hbar as a function of x = k R."""
        return (np.sqrt(x**2 - m**2) - m * np.arccos(m / x)) / np.pi


    def torus_levels(m, s_max):
        return np.array([brentq(lambda x: radial_action(x, m) - (s - 0.25), m + 1e-12, m + 4 * s + 10) for s in range(1, s_max + 1)]) / R

An orbit on a torus
-----------------------
A billiard orbit that keeps angular momentum :math:`m` bounces between
the wall and the caustic circle and, for irrational winding, covers the
whole annulus. The two independent loops on the torus are once around
the centre (:math:`\gamma_\theta`) and once out to the wall and back
(:math:`\gamma_r`).

    r_c = m / k

    step = np.pi - 2 * chi  # angle advanced per bounce

Torus quantization against the exact spectrum
-------------------------------------------------
Every level is labelled by its two torus quantum numbers :math:`(m,s)`.
The torus rule reproduces the Bessel zeros, and its error falls as
:math:`k` grows.
