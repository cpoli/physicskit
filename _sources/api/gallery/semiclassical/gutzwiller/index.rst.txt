The Gutzwiller trace formula
--------------------------------

The exact one-dimensional Gutzwiller trace formula, reconstructing a
spectrum purely from a single classical periodic orbit's action and
period, and the general isolated-orbit stability amplitude used beyond
the exactly-solvable 1D case; the quartic oscillator's spectrum and
revivals; and the Berry-Tabor length spectrum of an integrable billiard.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Gutzwiller&#x27;s trace formula assumes each periodic orbit is isolated. In an integrable system that fails. Periodic orbits come in continuous families filling whole resonant tori, and the Gutzwiller stability weight 1/\sqrt{|\det(M-I)|} diverges. Berry and Tabor (1976) replaced the sum over isolated orbits with a sum over these families, labelled by integer winding numbers (M, N). Each family adds an oscillation to the density of states with amplitude proportional to the area it sweeps and falling off only as L^{-1/2} with its length L.">
