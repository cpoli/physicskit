Semiclassical propagators
-----------------------------

The Van Vleck-Morette single-trajectory propagator, the Maslov index's
topological caustic-counting correction, and the Herman-Kluk
multi-trajectory frozen-Gaussian propagator, each checked against exact
quantum evolution of the harmonic oscillator.
