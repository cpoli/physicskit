Electrostatics and magnetostatics
------------------------------------

Static fields from charges and steady currents: Coulomb's inverse-square
law, the Laplace-Legendre multipole expansion, Poisson's equation solved
on a grid by relaxation and by fast transforms, Green's method of images
for conductors, and the Biot-Savart field of a current loop and a finite
solenoid.
