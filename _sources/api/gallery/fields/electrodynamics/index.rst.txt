Electrodynamics
------------------

Finite-difference time-domain (FDTD) solutions of Maxwell's equations on a
Yee grid, in 1D and 2D, plus a graded-conductivity absorbing boundary in
the spirit of Berenger's Perfectly Matched Layer. Also includes animated
demos of a Hertzian oscillating dipole antenna, a wave crossing a
dielectric slab, and a standing TM cavity mode.
