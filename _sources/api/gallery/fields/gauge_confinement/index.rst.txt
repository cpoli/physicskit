Gauge confinement
---------------------

A simplified, illustrative 1+1D toy model of quark confinement: a field
squeezed into a fixed-cross-section "flux tube" between two opposite
charges, reproducing the linearly growing confinement energy that Kenneth
Wilson's 1974 lattice gauge theory predicted -- not a lattice-QCD or
first-principles gauge-theory calculation.

The physical picture is that the gluon field lines are squeezed into a narrow &quot;flux tube&quot; of fixed cross-section connecting the two charges, rather than spreading out in all directions the way an ordinary Coulomb field would.">
