For a ray grazing the Sun that is 1.75 arcseconds. On 29 May 1919 expeditions to Sobral and Príncipe photographed the stars around the eclipsed Sun and compared their positions with night-time plates of the same field. The stars had shifted outward by the Einstein amount.">
